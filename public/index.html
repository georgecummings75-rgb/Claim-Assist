Claims fail for the same avoidable reasons

Most denials and delays don't come from the loss itself — they come from missing photos, no proof of value, or a packet that never made it to the counter in a usable form. By the time an associate notices what's missing, the customer has already left.

Claim Assist front-loads that work: the checklist is specific to the claim type, so nothing gets discovered after the fact.

How a claim moves through it

Four short steps, done once, at the time the problem is reported.

STEP 1

Describe it

Customer types what happened. The system reads it and identifies the claim type on its own.

STEP 2

Answer specifics

A short set of questions tailored to that claim type — packaging kept, tracking status, expected date.

STEP 3

Attach evidence

A checklist shows exactly what's needed, with photo upload built in — not a generic list.

STEP 4

Get the packet

A customer copy and a staff copy are generated, with a denial-risk flag if something's still missing.

CUSTOMER COPY

A packet they can actually submit

Plain-language summary of the incident, an itemized list of the evidence attached, and clear next steps — formatted and ready to print or hand over.

STAFF COPY

What your team needs at a glance

Claim type, tracking number, and a flagged list of anything still missing — plus a straight recommendation on whether it's ready to submit.
